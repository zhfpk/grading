Documentation of the grading application

TL;DR

  • This is a privacy-preserving app for grading e-exams.
  • It covers three stages of the exam lifecycle: grading, optional second-grader reassessment, and optional post-exam review by examinees.
  • Use group grading to apply one grading to identical answers across all examinees.
  • Click an examinee's name to open and grade their exam.
  • Choose to grade anonymously without seeing the examinee's real name.

Privacy-preserving grading

We only store the minimum data needed for examinees and graders. Graders receive random pseudonyms (German city names) instead of real names. It's the examiner's responsibility to control who gets access. For details on what data we store, see our privacy policy.

There is no automated decision-making in the sense of article 22 GDPR.

Exam Lifecycle

The exam moves through three phases, modelling its lifecycle (similar to those of paper-based exams).

  1. Grading phase: Grade submissions (points, comments, internal annotations). Only in this phase can grades be edited.
  2. Reassessment phase (optional): Failed students (grade 5.0) are reassessed by a second grader.
  3. Review phase (optional): Students receive links to review their exam results.

To switch phases, open Exam Lifecycle in the menu. You can move forward or back between phases once the phase-specific conditions are met. Hence, you may switch from the grading phase to the reassessment phase or the exam review phase. Additionally, you may always switch back to the grading phase from any phase.

Multiple exams

If you have been invited to more than one exam and want to grade them in parallel, you can switch between them using either the Your Exams link or your saved welcome links.

Only one exam can be open at a time. Attempting to access another exam without properly switching will result in a "Forbidden" error.

Grading

You can grade each student's full exam (similar to how you grade paper-based exams). Or use group grading to handle one question across all examinees at once.

Using individual grading

In individual grading, you grade per examinee. Use it to view the entire exam including the task descriptions and the submissions of a single examinee.

Using group grading

In group grading, you can focus on all answers to a single subtask question, i.e., a subtask field in which examinees write their answers. You see only examinee answers (not the question text), grouped by identical response. Apply one grading to each group to save time. Use filters (e.g., case-insensitive matching) to adjust grouping. For further information on filtering, see the help in the group grading view.

Switch between individual and group grading via the menu. In individual grading, click a field name to jump to its group view. In group grading, click a student's name to return to their individual exam.

Assigning points to answers

To assign points (positive integers only), enter your grading below each answer in the format:
<points> <optional comment> # <optional internal annotation>.

The points and the optional comment are visible to examinees when they inspect their exam results. The optional internal annotation is only visible to other graders (and not to examinees).

  • Example: "3" assigns three points.
  • Example: "2 correct" assigns two points with the comment "correct" (visible to examinees).
  • Example: "1 # needs review" assigns one point and adds the internal annotation "needs review" (only visible to graders).
  • Example: "5 partially correct # please verify" assigns five points with the comment "partially correct" (visible to examinees) and the internal annotation "please verify" (only visible to graders).
  • Example: "# unsure" adds an internal annotation (only visible to graders) without awarding points.

Your gradings are always automatically saved (indicated with a green border around the input field).

Adjusting the grading scale

In Points and Grades, set the score thresholds for each final grade. You can only edit the scale during the grading phase. Examinees need at least grade 4.0 to pass the exam.

Inviting other graders

To add colleagues as graders, go to the Graders page. Choose the grader role (defines their permissions). Click Create grader to generate an invitation link. Send that invitation link to your colleague - they can start grading once they accept.

Warning: Never share your personal invitation or welcome link! This link is meant for your eyes only!

{% endif %}

Reassessment

The grading application supports the reassessment by a second corrector. Only the exams of examinees that showed up and failed the exam (grade 5,0) have to be reassessed. As a reassessment grader, open Overview, click a failed exam, review the markings, and click Approve gradings to sign the current submissions and gradings with your private key.

{% if g.user.has_permission('access-ui:graders') %}

For reassessment, you must invite the second corrector. Select the Reassessment grader role. Ensure the exam phase is set to Reassessment.

{% endif %}

Post-exam review

The grading application allows examinees to review their exam results. Students use personal review links to see their results during the review period.

{% if g.user.has_permission('access-ui:manage-post-exam-review') %}

The examinees can only view their exam results during the review period. Configure the review period on the Post-Exam Review page. From there, download each examinee's link and distribute it to the examinees.

Security aspects

Ensuring confidentiality

All examinee submissions, their gradings, and personal data are encrypted. Decryption happens only in the user's browser. Only exam tasks remain unencrypted on the server.

Protecting integrity

We use authenticated encryption to prevent tampering. All grades and reassessments are signed with the grader's private key (generated client-side). The private key never leaves the browser.
