Categories of data

We store different data for different user roles.

For every page access, we store log files of our web server (timestamp, IP address, user agent, HTTP headers) to maintain operational security in accordance with Art. 6(1)(e) of the GDPR in conjunction with Art. 4 and 6 of the BayDSG and Art. 11 of the BayEGovG. The data is used to detect and prevent attacks and is deleted after 7 days.

For registered examinees of an electronic exam, we store full name, matriculation number, seat number, used laptop, final answers, points, grading comments, reassessment result, and grade. In case the examiner carries out the post-exam review using this service, we additionally store whether an examinee has accessed their exam results (as a yes/no value).

For graders participating in the grading of this exam, we store the real name entered by the person that invited the grader to an exam. This real name is only visible to other graders that are allowed to manage the grader's of the respective exam. Other users are not able to see a grader's real name, instead they only see the randomly chosen pseudonym (that is stored alongside the encrypted name on the server). Apart from a grader's real name (in encrypted form) and the associated pseudonym, we store the content entered into grading fields. We sign the content entered into grading fields using the asymmetric key pair, generated by accepting the grading invitation, and store the resulting signature. We store the public key of the generated key pair to link gradings to the grader's account and ensure their integrity and prevent malicious modifications. By storing the grader's public key, we can attribute gradings to the grader's account. For reassessments, we sign all visible gradings and submissions and store the resulting signature.

Recipients

The following recipients have access to the personal data processed during the examination and grading of the exam: employees at the Chair of Privacy and Security in Information Systems who are entrusted with the technical implementation of electronic examinations, examiners, graders with access to personal data, and, if necessary, members of examination bodies and the legal department. External disclosure only takes place as follows: if necessary, on an ad hoc basis to parties involved in legal proceedings.

During post-exam review, examinees of an electronic exam may access their own data (full name, matriculation number, seat number, used laptop, final answers, points, grading comments, reassessment result, and grade) as well as the pseudonyms and public keys of all graders. Using the public key, examinees can attribute their points and grading comments to the corresponding grader's pseudonym. Examinees do not learn the grader's identity (i.e., real name).

Retention period

Personal data relating to the examination will be stored as part of the archived examination for five years after the end of the year in which a student graduated.

Your rights

You have the following rights in relation to the processing of your data: Right to information (Art. 15 GDPR), Right to rectification (Art. 16 GDPR), Right to erasure (Art. 17 GDPR), Right to restriction of processing (Art. 18 GDPR), Right to data portability (Art. 20 GDPR), Right to object (Art. 21 GDPR), Right to complain to a supervisory authority (Art. 77 GDPR).
